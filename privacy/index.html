Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WeTube handles information when you use our YouTube channel management and publishing tools.

WeTube uses YouTube API Services. By using features that connect to YouTube, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and should review the Google Privacy Policy.

1. Information We process

When you connect a Google or YouTube account, WeTube may process account and channel information returned by Google APIs, such as your Google account identifier, basic profile information, YouTube channel identifiers, video metadata, playlist information, upload status, and other data needed to provide enabled features.

WeTube does not ask for, collect, or store your Google or YouTube password.

2. OAuth permissions

WeTube uses Google OAuth 2.0. Permissions are requested so WeTube can perform actions you choose, such as viewing authorized channel information, uploading videos, updating supported metadata, or managing related YouTube resources. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

3. How information is used

Information obtained from YouTube API Services is used only to provide, maintain, secure, and improve WeTube’s channel-management features, including displaying authorized channel data and performing user-requested publishing or management actions.

4. Storage and retention

WeTube may retain limited account identifiers, authorization-related data, settings, and API-derived data where needed to operate the service. Data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed, updated, or deleted as required by applicable YouTube API Services policies. We do not sell YouTube API data.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with service providers needed to operate WeTube, when required by law, or when you explicitly direct an action involving another service.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect stored information and authorization credentials from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

7. Your choices

You may disconnect WeTube from your Google Account, stop using the service, or request deletion of data associated with your account. See our Data Deletion & Revocation page for instructions.

8. Children

WeTube is not directed to children under the age required to independently consent to online services in their jurisdiction.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, use the contact method listed on our Contact page.
