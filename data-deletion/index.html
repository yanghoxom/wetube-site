Data Deletion & Revocation

Last updated: September 16, 2026

You can disconnect WeTube from your Google Account at any time and request deletion of data associated with your use of WeTube.

1. Revoke Google / YouTube access

Open your Google Account’s third-party connections or permissions page, locate WeTube, and remove its access. Revoking access prevents WeTube from making future authorized YouTube API requests on your behalf.

Google account permissions can be managed at myaccount.google.com/connections.

2. Request deletion from WeTube

To request deletion of stored account-related data, email wetube@lembem.com from the email address associated with your WeTube use. Use the subject WeTube Data Deletion Request.

Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant account or channel, such as the Google account email used with WeTube or the YouTube channel URL. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, recovery codes, or other secrets.

3. What is deleted

After a valid request is verified, we will delete stored personal information and account-related data that WeTube no longer needs to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate obligations.

4. API-derived data

Data obtained through YouTube API Services is handled in accordance with applicable YouTube API Services policies, including required refresh and deletion rules.

5. Need help?

Contact wetube@lembem.com or visit our Contact page.
